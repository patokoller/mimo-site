MIMO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

MIMO is published by Patrick Koller, Zurich, Switzerland. Contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL

The short version

MIMO has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. What you tell MIMO stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your taste answers, saved places, notes, day plans and the place you are staying. These are stored in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Your location, if you allow it. MIMO reads it only while the app is open, to show walking distances and to place you on the map. It is not stored and is never sent to us.

What leaves your phone, and to whom

MIMO has no server of its own. The app contacts three outside services to do its job. Each receives your IP address, as any internet service does, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

  • Apple Maps. Map images and the search for your hotel or address are provided by Apple. Your search text is sent to Apple.
  • Wikimedia Commons. Place photos and artwork are loaded from Wikimedia's servers.
  • Links you choose to open. Tapping "Google Maps", "Official site" or "Listing" opens that app or website, which then applies its own policy.

What we do not do

No tracking across apps or websites. No advertising identifiers. No third-party analytics or advertising software. No sale or sharing of personal data.

Children

MIMO is a travel guide for a general audience and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because MIMO holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your device: remove individual saves in the app, switch off location in iOS Settings, or delete the app to erase all of it.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
