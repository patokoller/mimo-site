MIMO Support

MIMO is a hand-checked city guide for Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka. For help, corrections or feedback, write to support@mimo.guide. We read every message.

A place is closed, has moved or has the wrong hours

Tell us the name of the place and what is wrong. Every place page shows the date its details were last checked. Corrections ship with the next app update.

The app is not showing my location

MIMO uses your location only when you are within 40 km of the city you selected. Elsewhere it measures distances from the city centre. To allow location, open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > MIMO and choose "While Using the App".

Photos do not load

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr and need an internet connection. Places without a licensed photo show a plain card instead.

I cannot find my hotel

Type the hotel's full name or its street address. The search covers the city you have selected, so switch city first if you are planning ahead.

How do I delete my data?

Everything MIMO knows about you is stored on your phone. Deleting the app removes it all. There is no account to close.

Suggest a place or a city

We add places slowly and check each one. Send the name and why you love it.
